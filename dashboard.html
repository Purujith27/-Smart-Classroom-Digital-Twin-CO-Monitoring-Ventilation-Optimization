Credibility and limitations

Model: single-zone CO₂ mass-balance approximation, 180 m³ room, outdoor CO₂ fixed at 420 ppm, 28 occupants during scheduled classes, one-minute time step and simulated sensor noise.

Intelligence: threshold controller plus sensor-jump flags; compared against low and high fixed-ventilation baselines.

Parameters are illustrative. The 1000 ppm line is only a reference for this demonstration, not a universal health or legal limit. CO₂ is not a complete measure of indoor air quality. Real deployment requires field calibration, validation and facilities/safety review.
